This section will detail management and grouping of devices. It will explain how to manage devices, connect them to channels, manage those channels, group devices and channels to groups and give specific people access to all these entities.

The platform mostly handles device and channel management from within a group as will be seen later on.

**Devices** can be a physical device or an application in your organization. **Groups** can be anything depending on the context of your organization. Similar case to **channels**. When connected to channels, devices are able to **publish** or **subscribe** to messages in that particular channel.
