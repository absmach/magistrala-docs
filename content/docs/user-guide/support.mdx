## Overview

The **Support** page lets you send a message directly to the Magistrala team from within the platform, without needing to look up a separate contact address.

To access it, click **Support** in the sidebar.

![Support Page](../img/support/support-page.png)

## Send a message

Fill in the **Subject** and **Message** fields, then click **Send Message**.

|Property |Description| Required |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|----------|
|Subject |A short summary of your question or issue| ✅ |
|Message |The full details of your question or issue| ✅ |

![Contact Form](../img/support/contact-form.png)

Once sent, you'll see a confirmation toast and be redirected back to your workspace. If your account email isn't verified yet, you're redirected to your [profile](/user-guide/profile-management) instead, where you can verify it.
